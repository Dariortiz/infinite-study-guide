MODULE 00

Category Theory

Learn universal properties, adjunctions, and limits as reusable mathematical interfaces.

Category Theory 1.1: Motivation and Philosophy
Bartosz Milewski · Category Theory for Programmers · 46 min

Milewski motivates morphisms and composition as reusable structure before the module turns to universal properties and Yoneda.

After watching: Translate one familiar construction into objects and arrows, then prove a universal property.
▶ Watch
Category Theory in Context
Emily Riehl · author PDF

The author's current PDF is the rigorous companion for Yoneda, limits, adjunctions, and diagram-level proofs.

After reading: Prove one construction by a universal property and formalize its uniqueness.
PDF
Exit artifact: Build a diagram-and-proof portfolio centered on universal properties.
MODULE 01

Schemes

Glue affine spectra and sheaves into the basic language of modern algebraic geometry.

Schemes 1: Introduction
Richard Borcherds · recorded algebraic geometry course · 28 min

Borcherds gives a focused entry from classical varieties to the scheme-theoretic viewpoint used by the written spine.

After watching: Compute Spec of three small rings and identify the geometric information carried by nilpotents.
▶ Watch
The Rising Sea — Parts II and III
Ravi Vakil · author notes

A detailed, motivated route to sheaves, schemes, morphisms, and geometry.

After reading: Compute Spec for three rings and glue two affine lines along an open subset.
PDF
Exit artifact: Compute explicit affine schemes, stalks, and one gluing construction.
MODULE 02

Malliavin Calculus

Differentiate random functionals and connect nondegeneracy to smooth densities.

Malliavin Calculus — Session 1
Erfan Salavati · recorded course · 60 min

The opening course session introduces differentiation on Wiener space at the pace needed for explicit cylindrical-functional calculations.

After watching: Compute Malliavin derivatives of three cylindrical functionals and verify one finite-dimensional integration-by-parts identity.
▶ Watch
The Malliavin Calculus and Related Topics — Chapters 1 and 2
David Nualart · Springer · 2nd ed.

Introduces the derivative, divergence, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck operator, and density criteria.

After reading: Calculate D of cylindrical functionals and prove the duality identity in a finite example.
Read
Exit artifact: Compute Malliavin derivatives for Wiener functionals and verify an integration-by-parts identity.
MODULE 03

Functional Analysis II

Continue into unbounded operators, spectral theory, distributions, and operator algebras.

Adjoints of Unbounded Operators
Roland Speicher · Mathematics of Quantum Mechanics · 34 min

This focused lecture makes domains and adjoints explicit—the distinctions the module's differential-operator artifact must preserve.

After watching: Compute the domains of a model differentiation operator and its adjoint, then test symmetry and self-adjointness.
▶ Watch
Methods of Modern Mathematical Physics I — Chapters VII and VIII
Reed & Simon · Academic Press

Develops spectral theory and unbounded operators with domains visible.

After reading: Determine closure, adjoint, and self-adjointness for a model differentiation operator.
Read
Exit artifact: Analyze one unbounded differential operator, including domain, adjoint, and spectrum.
MODULE 04

Complex Analysis II

Advance to Riemann surfaces, analytic continuation, and several complex variables.

Hartogs's Extension Theorem in Several Complex Variables
COMPASS Seminar · SCV lecture · 61 min

A theorem-focused lecture exposes the central several-variable phenomenon that has no one-variable analogue.

After watching: Prove a basic Hartogs extension case and identify exactly which dimension assumption is used.
▶ Watch
Riemann Surfaces: Algebra, Analysis, Geometry — Lecture 1
Norbert A'Campo · ICTS lecture series · 71 min

The lecture develops Riemann surfaces as the natural domains for analytic continuation and multivalued functions.

After watching: Construct the Riemann surface of a square root and compute its branch data.
▶ Watch
Lectures on Riemann Surfaces — Chapters 1–19
Otto Forster · Springer

A compact route from complex manifolds and sheaves to meromorphic functions.

After reading: Build the Riemann surface of a multivalued function and compute branch data.
Read
Exit artifact: Construct an analytic continuation or compact Riemann-surface example and prove its key property.
