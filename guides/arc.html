Grammar-based compression: combinatorics and algorithms
Markus Lohrey · AofA 2024 invited talk · 68 min

Lohrey is the main authority on grammar-based compression, and the talk sets straight-line grammars against the approximation bounds that make the greedy heuristics defensible.

After watching: State the smallest-grammar approximation bound from the talk and check your own implementation against it.
▶ Watch
Identifying Hierarchical Structure in Sequences
Nevill-Manning & Witten · arXiv

PDF
Exit artifact: Implement Sequitur or RePair and inspect grammar size on repetitive and random inputs.

Capstones & exit criteria

A finished route ends in an artifact you can run and a statement you can defend.

Inspectable compressor

  1. Combine reversible filtering, LZ parsing, and range or ANS entropy coding.
  2. Specify a framed binary format and deterministic decoder.
  3. Fuzz round trips and malformed inputs.
  4. Benchmark ratio, speed, peak memory, and latency on disclosed corpora.
