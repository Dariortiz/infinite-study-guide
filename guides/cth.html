After watching: Construct a samplable distribution and audit whether a reduction preserves it.
▶ Watch
Analyzing Average-Case Complexity by Meta-Complexity
Simons Institute · Meta-Complexity program · 62 min

Shows a modern use of average-case notions and forces careful separation of worst-case and distributional claims.

Arora–Barak — Chapter 18, Average-Case Complexity

Defines distributional problems, average-case classes, and reduction requirements.

Capstones & exit criteria

Cross-layer systems dossier

  1. Choose one protocol or security-sensitive primitive.
  2. State its hardness, timing, and failure assumptions.
  3. Measure cache, pipeline, ordering, and compiler behavior with reproducible evidence.
  4. Separate formal guarantees from empirical observations and point TEE-specific material to the existing TEE guide.
