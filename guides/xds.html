MODULE 00

KAM in Depth

Study persistence of invariant tori through small divisors and iterative normal forms.

KAM Theory and the Stability of the Solar System
Nils Berglund · KAM tutorial · 78 min

The tutorial connects Diophantine frequencies, perturbation size, and invariant-torus persistence to a concrete Hamiltonian system.

After watching: Solve one homological equation and visualize resonant gaps in a standard-map parameter sweep.
▶ Watch
A Lecture on the Classical KAM Theorem
Jürgen Pöschel · AMS proceedings

A concise proof route through Diophantine conditions and the iteration.

After reading: Carry out one homological-equation step and track the small-divisor estimate.
Read
Exit artifact: Work a finite KAM iteration for a near-integrable map and visualize surviving tori.
MODULE 01

Symplectic Geometry

Use symplectic manifolds, Hamiltonian vector fields, and moment maps as dynamics language.

Symplectic Geometry and Classical Mechanics — Lecture 1
Tobias Osborne · recorded lecture course · 85 min

Begins from Hamiltonian mechanics and derives the symplectic structures used throughout the geometric module.

After watching: Compute the Hamiltonian vector field for two explicit systems and verify flow preservation of the symplectic form.
▶ Watch
Lectures on Symplectic Geometry
Ana Cannas da Silva · Springer LNM 1764

A direct route from forms and Darboux to Hamiltonian actions and reduction.

After reading: Verify a map is symplectic, derive its Hamiltonian field, and compute a moment map.
Read
Exit artifact: Compute Hamiltonian flows and symplectic reductions for explicit examples.
MODULE 02

Conley Theory

Describe global dynamics with isolating neighborhoods and index invariants.

Conley Theory and the Global Dynamics of Games
Fields Institute · research lecture · 33 min

A concrete application shows how isolating neighborhoods and Conley-type invariants encode global behavior beyond local linearization.

After watching: Construct an isolating neighborhood for a planar flow and compute the homology of a candidate index pair.
▶ Watch
Computational Homology
Kaczynski, Mischaikow & Mrozek · Springer

Connects cubical homology and rigorous computation to Conley-index methods.

After reading: Build a cubical enclosure for a map and calculate homology of an index pair.
Read
Exit artifact: Compute a Conley index for a planar isolated invariant set and validate it numerically.
MODULE 03

Navier–Stokes as Dynamics

Treat dissipative PDE evolution, absorbing sets, and attractors as an infinite-dimensional flow.

Mathematical Aspects of Turbulence — Part I
Theodore Drivas · Simons Center minicourse · 88 min

The minicourse frames Navier–Stokes through energy transfer and turbulent dynamics, supplying the analytic interpretation needed for the spectral artifact.

After watching: Implement a dealiased 2D pseudospectral solver and audit its discrete energy and enstrophy balances.
▶ Watch
Infinite-Dimensional Dynamical Systems — selected chapters
Roger Temam · Springer

Supplies semigroup, dissipativity, and global-attractor language for PDE dynamics.

After reading: Prove an absorbing-ball estimate for a simplified dissipative equation.
Read
Exit artifact: Implement a spectral 2D flow and estimate energy/enstrophy balances and attractor diagnostics.
MODULE 04

Complex Dynamics in Depth

Move from iteration pictures to normal families, Julia sets, and parameter space.

Complex Dynamics — Lecture 1
Juliette Bruce · MMUSSL lecture · 59 min

A full introductory lecture moves from iteration to Fatou and Julia behavior rather than stopping at rendered fractals.

After watching: Render a polynomial Julia set and prove complete invariance for the same map.
▶ Watch
Dynamics in One Complex Variable — Chapters 4–14
John Milnor · Princeton · 3rd ed.

The standard rigorous route through rational dynamics and Julia sets.

After reading: Prove complete invariance of the Julia set and implement interval-guarded escape tests.
Read
Exit artifact: Render certified escape sets and prove one structural statement about Julia/Fatou behavior.
