Capstones & exit criteria

A finished route ends in an artifact you can run and a statement you can defend.

Discrete systems dossier

  1. Choose one interval, one circle, and one planar map.
  2. Classify fixed and periodic points, compute multipliers or Lyapunov spectra, and identify invariant sets.
  3. Track one fold, flip, Neimark–Sacker, or border-collision bifurcation with a normal-form argument.
  4. Separate every theorem, floating-point observation, and validated numerical claim.

Coding an orbit

  1. Choose a map admitting a symbolic description.
  2. Construct or approximate a partition.
  3. Compare orbit growth with word growth and entropy.
  4. Document failures of the coding at boundaries.

Statistical dynamics audit

  1. Construct a transfer operator and an Ulam approximation for one expanding or hyperbolic map.
  2. Compute an invariant density, correlation decay, Lyapunov data, and an equilibrium or physical measure.
  3. Compare ensemble evolution with long-orbit statistics and state the ergodic hypotheses.
  4. Attach convergence evidence or rigorous enclosures to every numerical quantity.

Dynamical atlas

  1. Choose four families with at least two discrete examples and genuinely different invariant structure.
  2. Define each state space, evolution rule, and invariant object.
  3. Build one trustworthy visualization per family.
  4. Explain which properties survive perturbation.
