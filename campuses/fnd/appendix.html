MODULE 01

Placement & Diagnostics

Use short proof and build prompts to reveal gaps; no score is a credential and no familiar-looking theorem counts as mastery.

Analysis placement: compactness, convergence, and measure
Placement · points into ANA · 60–90 min

Tests whether the forgotten-bachelor's analysis language is actually available for later process and dynamics proofs.

After reading: Without notes: prove a compactness consequence, separate pointwise from uniform convergence, and justify one limit/integral interchange.
Open ANA
Probability placement: information, martingales, and weak limits
Placement · points into PRB · 60–90 min

Checks I3 specifically. Undergraduate computations—including MIT 6.041 material already housed in PRE—are only the entry condition.

After reading: Define E[X|G] by its two properties, prove a stopped-martingale statement with hypotheses, and distinguish convergence in law from probability.
Open PRB
Algebra placement: quotients, modules, and spectra
Placement · points into ALG · 60–90 min

Detects whether structure proofs and spectral linear algebra are strong enough for arithmetic, coding, and motion.

After reading: Prove one quotient is well defined, classify a small module or operator, and explain eigenvalue sensitivity for a nonnormal matrix.
Open ALG
Computing placement: stability, reductions, and machines
Placement · points into CSF · 60–90 min

Separates ordinary programming familiarity from numerical and theoretical computing literacy.

After reading: Distinguish conditioning from stability, give one mapping reduction with an iff proof, and implement a small automaton or stable QR test.
Open CSF
Exit artifact: Complete a timed diagnostic packet and annotate every miss as definition, theorem choice, proof construction, or implementation evidence.

MODULE 03

Guide Readiness

Enter each guide only when its prerequisite evidence is present; exit the appendix as soon as routing is clear.

ANA readiness card
G1 · Analysis · guide pointer

ANA is ready when elementary proofs and calculus are available but compactness, measure, operators, or Fourier arguments are not.

After reading: Attempt ANA's first real-analysis artifact for 45 minutes; continue only if the proof gaps are structural rather than notation recall.
Open ANA
PRB readiness card
G2 · Probability Language · guide pointer

PRB begins after undergraduate probability placement and supplies conditional expectation, martingales, and weak convergence as I3.

After reading: Write a probability-space model and conditional expectation from definitions; if only density formulas are available, begin PRB.
Open PRB
ALG readiness card
G3 · Algebra & Spectra · guide pointer

ALG is appropriate when matrix computation is familiar but quotient, module, or invariant-subspace proofs are weak.

After reading: Solve one homomorphism/quotient problem and one spectral proof; route only the failed half.
Open ALG
CSF readiness card
G4 · Computing Foundations · guide pointer

CSF is not introductory programming; it begins at numerical error and formal models of computation.

After reading: Explain backward stability and write one reduction. If either explanation is slogan-only, route to that CSF module.
Open CSF
Exit artifact: Create four readiness cards, each with prerequisites, a cold-start task, and an explicit stop/continue rule.
MODULE 04

Cross-Bundle Bridges

Use Foundations as feeder infrastructure: point to the consuming campus and avoid repeating its subject matter here.

Bridge to Motion: ANA + PRB + spectral ALG
MOT · DYN/STO/SPZ/DSZ/SIM · bundle pointer

Motion consumes compactness and fixed points, conditional expectation and weak limits, spectra, and stable numerics.

After reading: For your first Motion guide, list the exact ANA/PRB/ALG/CSF artifacts it assumes and close only demonstrated gaps.
Open MOT
Bridge to Arithmetic and Information
ART/INF · bundle pointers

Arithmetic primarily consumes proof algebra and spectra; Information consumes probability language, automata, and numerical experimentation.

After reading: Choose one target guide and trace a minimal prerequisite path without adding unrelated optional modules.
Open ART
Bridge to Privacy and Systems through PRE
PRE → PRV/SYS · existing crypto prerequisite pointer

Existing crypto guides already own their applied prerequisites and should not be reconstructed in FNA.

After reading: Audit one intended crypto guide against PRE; add ANA/PRB/ALG/CSF only where that guide's mathematics or verification work truly demands it.
Open PRE

Capstones & exit criteria

  2. Attach evidence to every skip and a diagnostic miss to every study choice.
  3. Choose the smallest coherent first route and a date to reassess it.

Dependency matrix

  1. Select intended downstream guides across the campuses.
  2. Map each prerequisite to an existing PRE, ANA, PRB, ALG, or CSF artifact.
  3. Delete duplicated study and identify optional modules with no current consumer.
